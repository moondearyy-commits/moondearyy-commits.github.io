Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

clipzilla-rclone is a personal tool used only by its owner, together with the rclone command-line program, to access the owner's own Google Drive.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, share or transfer any user data. The app has no servers and no database. Authorization tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration file on the owner's own computer.

Google user data

The app accesses Google Drive files only on the owner's request, and only to sync them between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own devices. Google user data is not shared with any third party.
